# Branding & Logo Typography Reference

Typography for wordmarks, brand identities, logotypes, and packaging. Explains how brand typography differs from UI and document typography.

---

## 1. Logo Typography vs. UI Typography

| Dimension | Website / UI Typography | Brand / Logo Typography |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Goal** | Sustained reading endurance & fast scanning | Instant visual recognition & memorability |
| **Glyph Count** | Thousands of continuous words | 1 to 4 focal words |
| **Aperture & Details**| Open apertures to avoid clogging at 12px | Distinctive, proprietary, or customized quirks |
| **Kerning** | Algorithmic font metrics table | Manual optical tracking, glyph-by-glyph |
| **Scalability** | Readable from 12px to 24px | Identifiable from a 16px favicon to a billboard |

---

## 2. Key Criteria for Logo Typeface Evaluation

1. **Distinctive Silhouette**:
   - The brand name must have a recognizable visual contour even when blurred or viewed in silhouette.
   - Avoid generic, overused neutral fonts for logotypes unless paired with an iconic mark.
2. **Short-Word Visual Balance**:
   - Some fonts look magnificent in long sentences but awkward in short uppercase abbreviations (e.g. wide tracking discrepancies).
   - Test target brand acronyms and exact casing.
3. **Customization Potential**:
   - Professional brand identities rarely leave a typeface unmodified. Designers alter crossbars, tighten ligatures, or customize terminals.
   - Typefaces with strong geometric skeletons (`Chillax`, `Neuropol`, `Reckoner`, `Aclonica`) provide great foundations for vector customization.
4. **Trademark & Commercial Vector Outlines**:
   - Verify that the font's EULA permits using font glyphs converted to vector outlines (`.svg`/`.eps`) for commercial trademark registration.
   - SIL Open Font License 1.1 explicitly permits this; proprietary freeware licenses may restrict trademarking font letterforms.

## 3. Brand Archetype Alignment

- **Quiet Luxury / Minimalist**: Light/medium geometric sans (`Chillax`) with wide uppercase tracking (`+0.12em`).
- **High Fashion / Haute Couture**: High-contrast modern serif (`Ithaca`, `Credit Valley`).
- **Futuristic / Deep Tech**: Extended techno-geometric cuts (`Neuropol`, `Achtung Bravo`).
- **Playful / Lifestyle**: Organic rounded or character-driven cuts (`Aclonica`, `Balhattan`).
