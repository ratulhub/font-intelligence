# Document & Editorial Typography Reference (Word, PDF, Reports, Resumes)

Typography for long-form print and digital documents, academic whitepapers, annual reports, resumes, and digital PDFs.

---

## 1. Document Page Geometry & Measure

- **Body Font Size**: **10pt to 11.5pt** for print documents; **11pt to 12pt** for documents intended primarily for on-screen PDF viewing.
- **Line Length**: **60 to 75 characters per line** (`measure`). In multi-column reports, reduce measure to **40 to 50 characters**.
- **Line Spacing (Leading)**:
  - Single-column reports: **1.25x to 1.35x font size** (approx. 13.5pt to 15pt leading for 11pt text).
  - Dense legal / academic documents: **1.2x font size**.

---

## 2. Document Hierarchy Standards

| Section | Role | Size | Leading | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Document Title | H1 | 24pt – 30pt | 28pt – 34pt | Clean optical weight; centered or flush left |
| Major Heading | H2 | 16pt – 18pt | 20pt – 22pt | Prominent section divider with keep-with-next |
| Subsection | H3 | 12pt – 13pt | 16pt | Bold lead-in |
| Body Text | Body | 10.5pt – 11pt| 14.5pt – 15pt | High legibility serif or neutral neo-grotesque |
| Footnotes / Meta| Small | 8.5pt – 9pt | 11pt | Sized down by 2pt minimum |
| Running Header | Meta | 8pt – 8.5pt | 10pt | Small caps or letterspaced uppercase |

---

## 3. Resume & CV Typography

- **The 6-Second Scan**: Recruiters scan resumes in 6 seconds. Clear weight deltas (700 bold section titles vs 400 normal body bullets) are mandatory.
- **ATS (Applicant Tracking System) Compatibility**: Standard PDF export with embedded TrueType outlines ensures automated parsers extract text accurately without character corruption.
- **Recommended Resume Typefaces**:
  - `General Sans` (Modern tech, product, marketing)
  - `Credit Valley` (Executive, legal, finance, academia)
  - `Ubuntu` (Engineering, DevOps, data science)
